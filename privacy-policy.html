Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this Tax Calculator application handles and protects user information.

Data Privacy Assurance

All inputs (salary, deductions, PAN) are processed 100% locally on your device. No financial data is uploaded or stored on external servers.

1. Information Entered

All values remain within your browser environment. No login credentials or remote accounts are created.

2. Data Storage & Sharing

Data entered is never sold, shared, or transferred to third-party databases or marketing organizations.
