Privacy Policy & Data Security

🔒 100% On-Device & Zero Cloud Storage

This Privacy Policy explains how AKC Tax Calculator (“the App”), owned and operated by Anurag Karundia & Co., handles and protects user information.

Privacy Assurance

Your financial inputs (salary, deductions, PAN) are processed 100% locally on your device. We do not store or transmit your sensitive tax data to any external server.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide accurate tax calculations and generate official computation statements, users may voluntarily enter:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • PAN Number
  • Salary & Investment Details

This information is used strictly in real-time within the App to calculate tax liabilities and prepare printable schedules. No account creation or password is required.

2. Data Storage & Sharing

All calculations are performed locally in the user's browser runtime. Anurag Karundia & Co. does not store, sell, trade, or share your data with any third party.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

The App does not sell personal records to marketing agencies, does not display commercial banner advertisements, and does not sell user habits.

4. Data Security

Because no taxpayer records are uploaded to remote databases, the risk of data compromise or cloud breach is virtually eliminated.
