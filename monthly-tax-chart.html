📌 Important Note

Annual increments, DA arrears, and bonus amounts are not included in the gross salary above. Consequently, monthly income tax deductions will adjust whenever there is a variation in your monthly salary.

💡 Practical Example

If an employee's monthly gross salary in March 2026 is ₹1,07,000, the monthly tax deduction is ₹1,000. However, if their monthly salary increases to ₹1,10,000 in any subsequent month, the monthly tax deduction will become ₹4,000 (or adjusted per slab).
